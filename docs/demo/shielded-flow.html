ZEC Perps - Shielded order flow

DEMO

A self-contained visualization of the commit-reveal mechanism, using the same SHA-256 commitment as app/shielded/commitment.ts. No wallet, no chain - it shows how an order stays hidden until its reveal window closes.

Place a shielded order

On seal, the client computes commitment = SHA-256(order || salt) and publishes only that. The order's side/price/size stay hidden until reveal.

Sealed orders

No sealed orders yet. Seal one to watch the lifecycle.

What this demonstrates (honestly)

  • Pre-trade secrecy real - during [seal, reveal) only the commitment is public; the order is off the book. At reveal it becomes a normal, public Injective order.
  • Integrity real - anyone can recompute SHA-256(revealed order || salt) and check it equals the earlier commitment (shown per order below).
  • Identity unlinkability relayer mode - in the app, a relayer executes the revealed order from a pooled account; not shown here.
  • Post-trade position privacy not provided - Injective positions/funding/liquidations stay public; real per-trader privacy needs a shielded pool with zk accounting (roadmap).
